## How to read these figures (front matter, p. 3)

> A download in this report is a single successful file retrieval recorded by a member repository's
> server log, filtered for known robots using the COUNTER-style exclusion list described in
> Appendix B.
>
> **A download is a retrieval event. It is not evidence of readership or use.** We cannot determine
> whether a retrieved file was opened, read in whole or in part, cited, or acted upon. A single
> reader may generate many retrievals; many readers may share one retrieval. Automated retrieval that
> our exclusion list does not recognise remains in these counts.
>
> We also cannot characterise who retrieved a file. Our logs carry no affiliation, sector,
> occupation, or geographic attribution beyond coarse network-level information, which we do not
> report. Statements about academic versus non-academic audiences cannot be supported by these data.
>
> Comparisons between access categories in section 4 describe retrieval counts only.
