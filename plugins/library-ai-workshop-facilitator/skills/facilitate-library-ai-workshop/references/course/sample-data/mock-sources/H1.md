**Green versus gold: archiving practices in health sciences**

Novak, T.

*Repository Studies*, vol. 5, no. 1 (2023), pp. 44–61
Article type: research article

## Abstract

We compare two routes to open availability for health sciences literature: deposit of an accepted
manuscript in a repository, and publication in an openly licensed journal. Using deposit records from
19 institutional repositories and publisher metadata for 22,000 health sciences articles published
2018–2022, we describe deposit rates by discipline, the length of embargo actually applied, version
discrepancies between the repository copy and the published article, and the proportion of articles
available by either route. Deposit rates varied widely by department. Median embargo length was
shorter than publisher policy required in a substantial minority of cases, and 6% of deposited
records pointed to a version that differed materially from the published article. We describe the
metadata practices that made version identification possible and those that defeated it.

**Keywords:** open access, institutional repositories, self-archiving, embargoes, version control
