# WEXFORD THESAURUS — EXTRACT — SIMULATED WORKSHOP MATERIAL

> **What this file is.** An extract from the controlled vocabulary of a database that does not exist.
> The descriptors, scope notes, identifiers, and hierarchy below were invented for this workshop.
> They are not the terms of any real thesaurus and they must not be assigned to a real record.
>
> **This extract is complete for the two branches it covers** — *Scholarly communication* and
> *Public health* — as of the 2026 release, together with the subject-authority terms used by the
> Calderwood Institute Archives at the end of the file. A term in one of these subject areas that
> does not appear here is not a descriptor in this release. That is a finding, not a gap in the
> extract.
>
> Do not cite this file.

## Branch: Scholarly communication

### Scholarly communication
`WT-01000`
SN: The full range of formal and informal processes by which research is registered, certified,
disseminated, and preserved. Use for the system as a whole. For a specific publishing model, use the
appropriate narrower term.
NT: Open access publishing · Peer review · Preprints · Research data sharing · Journal metrics
RT: Research evaluation

### Open access publishing
`WT-01100`
SN: Publishing models in which the published version of a research output is available to readers
without payment. Covers publisher-side models only. For author deposit of manuscripts in a
repository, use *Repository deposit*.
BT: Scholarly communication
NT: Article processing charges · Hybrid open access · Diamond open access
UF: Open-access journals · Free access publishing · Freely available publishing
RT: Repository deposit · Publishing economics

### Article processing charges
`WT-01110`
SN: Fees payable by authors, institutions, or funders for publication in an open access or hybrid
venue. Use for the charge itself and for policies governing it, not for library acquisitions budgets
generally.
BT: Open access publishing
UF: APCs · Author-side fees

### Hybrid open access
`WT-01120`
SN: Subscription venues in which individual items are made openly available on payment of a fee.
BT: Open access publishing

### Diamond open access
`WT-01130`
SN: Open access publishing carrying no charge to author or reader. Introduced in the 2024 release.
BT: Open access publishing
UF: Platinum open access

### Repository deposit
`WT-01200`
SN: Deposit of manuscripts, published versions, or data in an institutional or subject repository.
Use for the act and for policies mandating it. Not for the repository software.
BT: Scholarly communication
UF: Green open access · Self-archiving
RT: Open access publishing

### Preprints
`WT-01300`
SN: Research manuscripts made public before formal peer review. Do not use for accepted manuscripts
after peer review; for those use *Repository deposit*.
BT: Scholarly communication

### Journal metrics
`WT-01400`
SN: Quantitative indicators calculated at the level of a journal or serial title.
BT: Scholarly communication
UF: Impact factor · Journal impact indicators
RT: Bibliometrics

### Bibliometrics
`WT-01500`
SN: Quantitative study of publications and citations. Use for methods and for studies applying them.
For assessment of individuals or institutions using such methods, use *Research evaluation*.
NT: Citation analysis · Altmetrics · Download statistics
RT: Journal metrics · Research evaluation

### Citation analysis
`WT-01510`
SN: Analysis of citation relationships between publications.
BT: Bibliometrics

### Altmetrics
`WT-01520`
SN: Indicators drawn from social media, news, policy documents, and other non-citation sources. The
descriptor covers the indicators; it does not imply that they measure readership or use.
BT: Bibliometrics

### Download statistics
`WT-01530`
SN: Counts of file requests recorded by a publisher, repository, or platform. Not evidence of
reading, and not to be indexed as readership.
BT: Bibliometrics
UF: Usage counts · Full-text requests

### Research evaluation
`WT-01600`
SN: Assessment of research outputs, individuals, groups, or institutions, whether for funding,
promotion, or accountability.
UF: Research assessment · Research quality assessment
RT: Bibliometrics · Research policy

### Research policy
`WT-01700`
SN: Government, funder, and institutional policy governing the conduct, funding, and dissemination of
research.
RT: Research evaluation · Health policy

## Branch: Public health

### Public health
`WT-02000`
SN: Organised measures to prevent disease and promote health at population level. Use for the field
and its practice. For the health status of a population as an outcome, use *Population health*.
NT: Health promotion · Disease surveillance · Environmental health · Health equity
UF: Community health · Public health practice
RT: Health policy · Population health

### Population health
`WT-02050`
SN: The health outcomes of a defined group, and their distribution within it.
RT: Public health · Health equity

### Health promotion
`WT-02100`
SN: Activity intended to increase control over health at individual or community level.
BT: Public health

### Disease surveillance
`WT-02200`
SN: Systematic collection and interpretation of health data for public health action. Covers
notifiable-disease reporting and syndromic systems.
BT: Public health
UF: Epidemiological surveillance

### Environmental health
`WT-02300`
SN: Health effects of physical, chemical, and biological factors external to the person.
BT: Public health

### Health equity
`WT-02400`
SN: The absence of avoidable and unjust differences in health between population groups. Use for
studies of the differences and for measures addressing them.
BT: Public health
UF: Health disparities · Health inequalities
RT: Population health

### Health policy
`WT-02500`
SN: Policy governing health services, public health measures, and their financing.
NT: Health services financing
RT: Public health · Research policy · Evidence-based policy

### Evidence-based policy
`WT-02600`
SN: Use of research evidence in the formulation of policy. Use for the practice and for studies of
it. Not for the evidence itself.
RT: Health policy · Research evaluation

### Knowledge translation
`WT-02700`
SN: Processes by which research findings are moved into practice, including exchange, synthesis, and
application. Introduced in the 2022 release.
RT: Evidence-based policy · Health promotion

## Subject-authority terms — Calderwood Institute Archives

The Institute assigns subject access points from this list. Deprecated forms are retained here so
that legacy description can be identified; they must not be assigned to new description.

### Urban renewal
`WT-04100`
SN: Publicly directed clearance, redevelopment, or rehabilitation of built-up areas. Use for the
programmes and for records documenting them.
UF: Slum clearance · Blight removal · Redevelopment (urban)
RT: Housing policy · Displacement (population)

### Displacement (population)
`WT-04150`
SN: Removal of residents from an area, whether by programme, purchase, or condemnation. Use for the
removal and its administration.
RT: Urban renewal · Housing policy

### Housing policy
`WT-04200`
SN: Policy governing the supply, condition, tenure, and allocation of housing.
RT: Urban renewal

### Municipal engineering
`WT-04300`
SN: Design, construction, and maintenance of public works by a municipal authority.
NT: Harbour engineering · Sanitary engineering
RT: City planning

### City planning
`WT-04400`
SN: Public planning of land use and the built environment.
RT: Municipal engineering · Urban renewal

### African Americans
`WT-04500`
SN: Use for persons and communities of African descent in the United States, and for records
documenting them.

### Colored people
`WT-04501` — **DEPRECATED 1998**
Do not assign. USE: African Americans

### Negro districts
`WT-04502` — **DEPRECATED 1998**
Do not assign. No replacement descriptor has been established in this vocabulary. Where a specific
community or place name is known, assign the place name and describe the community in the scope note.
