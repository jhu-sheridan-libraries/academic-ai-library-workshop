SIMULATED AND DE-IDENTIFIED READING ROOM INQUIRY

Everything below is fictional workshop material. The Calderwood Institute, Weldon Harbor, the
Tidal Flats, and every person and collection named here are invented. Nothing in this file
describes a real repository, a real collection, or a real person.

Received by: Calderwood Institute Archives and Special Collections, reading room reference queue
Channel: web form, "Ask an Archivist"
Requester: Doctoral candidate, urban history, another institution
Requester's stated affiliation and department are on file; they are not reproduced here.

---

Request as submitted:

"I am finishing a dissertation chapter on the displacement of households from the Tidal Flats
neighborhood during the Weldon Harbor waterfront redevelopment, and I would like to see everything
you have on it. I am especially interested in the papers of Ada Ferris, who I understand was the
central figure in the opposition to the clearance. My manuscript is due to the press in five weeks.

I can come to Weldon Harbor for two days, ideally in the next fortnight, but I would rather work
from digital copies if that is faster. Could you send me scans of the relevant material? I need
600 dpi TIFFs of about twenty photographs for a small online exhibit that will accompany the
chapter, plus permission to publish them. My press needs the permissions letter with the
manuscript.

I looked at your online finding aid and it seems fairly complete, so I think I know roughly what
is there. If you could just pull the folders that relate to the Tidal Flats and let me know which
photographs are usable, that would be enormously helpful."

Staff intake notes:

Deliverable: a dissertation chapter and an accompanying online exhibit of roughly twenty
photographic images, with publication permissions.

Deadline: manuscript to the press in five weeks. Requester proposes a two-day visit within two
weeks.

Access and reproduction need: publication-quality reproductions (600 dpi TIFF) and written
permission to publish, for material the requester has not yet identified item by item.

Known ambiguity: "materials on the Tidal Flats displacement" does not name a unit of description.
The subject is documented across at least three different kinds of holding here — the personal
papers of a family involved in city government, the organizational records of the body that
carried out the clearance, and a photographic survey created for the clearance itself. These are
different collections, in different states of processing, with different creators, different
access conditions, and different rights situations. They are not interchangeable, and the
requester's question does not yet say which body of evidence the chapter needs.

Points that will need correcting before a search plan is agreed: the requester's assumption that
one named individual's papers are the relevant unit; the assumption that the online finding aid
represents the whole of what the repository holds on the subject; the assumption that the material
is already digitized and can be sent by email; and the assumption that this repository is in a
position to grant permission to publish every photograph in its custody.
