## Established records

### WHA-N-00412

`EF` **Ferris, Harlan, 1889–1968**
`SN` City Engineer of Weldon Harbor, 1931–1957. Draughtsman and then assistant engineer in the city
engineer's office from 1911. Record established 1974 from municipal payroll registers and the city
directory; date of retirement corrected 2011 from the council minutes, which record his successor
taking office in 1957.
`VF` Ferris, H. (Harlan)
`VF` Ferris, Harlan W.
`VF` Ferris, Harlan Whitcomb, 1889–1968
`SA` Weldon Harbor. Office of the City Engineer

### WHA-N-00415

`EF` **Ferris, Ada Whitcomb, 1897–1979**
`SN` Civic activist and clubwoman, Weldon Harbor. Married Harlan Ferris in 1921. Not to be confused
with her daughter, also named Ada Ferris, for whom no record has been established. Record established
1988 from obituaries and the records of the Weldon Harbor Women's Civic League.
`VF` Whitcomb, Ada, 1897–1979
`VF` Ferris, Ada W.
`VF` Ferris, Mrs. Harlan

### WHA-C-00087

`EF` **Weldon Harbor. Office of the City Engineer**
`SN` Established by city ordinance 1904. Responsible for municipal survey, public works design, and
construction supervision. Abolished 1971; functions transferred to the Department of Public Works.
The office is entered subordinately to the city; do not enter it directly as "Office of the City
Engineer."
`VF` Weldon Harbor City Engineer's Office
`VF` City Engineer of Weldon Harbor
`SA` Weldon Harbor. Department of Public Works
`SA` Ferris, Harlan, 1889–1968

### WHA-C-00092

`EF` **Weldon Harbor. Department of Public Works**
`SN` Established 1971, assuming the functions of the Office of the City Engineer and of the
Department of Streets. Still active at the date of this extract.
`SA` Weldon Harbor. Office of the City Engineer

### WHA-C-00131

`EF` **Weldon Harbor Harbor Commission**
`SN` Independent commission established by state act 1911 to administer the port and its properties.
Dissolved 1968; records held in part by the Calderwood Institute. Not a city department; do not enter
subordinately to Weldon Harbor.
`VF` Harbor Commission of Weldon Harbor
`VF` Weldon Harbor Port Commission

`VF` Women's Civic League (Weldon Harbor)
