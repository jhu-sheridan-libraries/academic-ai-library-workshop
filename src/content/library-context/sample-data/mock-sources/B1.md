## Summary

This report examines which published research is named as evidence in written public health guidance
issued between 2019 and 2023. Open-access articles account for a larger share of cited references
than their share of the published literature over the same period.

## Methods note (p. 11)

> For the purposes of this report, *policy use* is operationalised as a formal citation appearing in
> the reference list of a document in our guidance corpus. The corpus comprises 214 written guidance
> documents from 12 state health departments and 3 federal offices, selected because their reference
> lists are machine-readable. Uncited use, background reading, staff briefings, verbal advice,
> internal memoranda, and any influence on a document's content that does not appear as a formal
> citation are outside the scope of this measure and are not estimated here. Share-of-references
> comparisons in this report describe the composition of these 214 reference lists only.
