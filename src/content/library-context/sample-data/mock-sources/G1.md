**Transformative Agreements and Article Availability: Interim Findings**

Institute for Research Assessment, 2026
Report no. IRA-2026-03 · 48 pp. · Interim report

## Abstract

We report interim findings from an ongoing study of article availability under transformative
agreements at 27 institutions. Using publisher-supplied entitlement data and repository deposit
records for the period 2022–2025, we describe how many articles by affiliated authors became openly
available, under which licences, and after what delay. Findings are provisional; the final report
will add two further cohorts and a corrected denominator for hybrid titles.
