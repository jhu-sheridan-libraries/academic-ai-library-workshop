**Access to Health Research**

World Health Assembly briefing note, 2022
Document WHA/BN/2022/14 · 6 pp. · Category: briefing note for delegates
Languages: English, French, Spanish

## Purpose

To inform delegates, ahead of the agenda item on research capacity, of current positions and
existing commitments concerning access to published health research. This note does not propose a
resolution and carries no decision text.

## Contents

1. Background: existing commitments on research access, 2013–2021
2. Summary of member state positions as reported to the secretariat
3. Programmes providing discounted or waived access in eligible countries
4. Matters delegates may wish to consider
