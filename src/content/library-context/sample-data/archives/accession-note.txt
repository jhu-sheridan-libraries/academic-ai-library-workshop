Repository: Calderwood Institute Archives and Special Collections, Weldon Harbor
Accession number: 2026-017
Received: 14 February 2026, by truck, on pallets
Recorded by: processing archivist (initials withheld for the workshop)

---

SOURCE

Transferred by the Weldon Harbor Office of Community Development, which describes itself as the
successor body to the former Weldon Harbor Redevelopment Authority. The Authority was dissolved in
1974. Whether the Office of Community Development holds the authority to convey these records, or
merely custody of them, has not been established.

CONTENT

Records of the Weldon Harbor Redevelopment Authority, chiefly 1949–1974, relating to the
acquisition, clearance, and redevelopment of the lower city, including the Tidal Flats. Includes
minutes, project files, property acquisition and appraisal files, relocation case files,
correspondence, engineering files, photographic negatives and prints, and public information
material.

EXTENT

212 linear feet on 47 pallets, measured on arrival.
Plus approximately 40 record cartons received separately in March and not yet measured.
Plus 14 reels of 16mm microfilm, extent not recorded.
Plus 6 flat files of oversize maps and plats, extent not recorded.

No box list accompanied roughly 60 of the cartons. A partial transfer list covers the remainder and
is at folder level for some boxes and box level for others.

CONDITION

Six cartons show water staining and one has visible mold; these have been isolated pending a
conservation assessment. Two pallets were shrink-wrapped over damp cardboard.

FORMATS OTHER THAN PAPER

- 3.5-inch diskettes, approximately 180, mostly unlabeled
- 2 Iomega Zip disks
- 1 reel of 8mm film, no can label
- 9 VHS cassettes, labeled "public meetings"
- 1 external hard drive, no label, provenance not established, not yet examined
- 14 reels of 16mm microfilm, silver halide, no reader currently on site for this format

The Institute has no current capacity to read the diskettes, the Zip disks, or the 8mm film. The
hard drive has not been connected to anything and should not be until it has been imaged on
isolated equipment.

DONOR CONDITIONS AS STATED IN CORRESPONDENCE

1. The transferring office asks that "personnel and case files" be closed for twenty-five years
   from the date of the record. The transfer list does not identify which boxes contain them.
2. The transferring office asks to review any publication drawing on the records prior to its
   release, and to be consulted on any request from a journalist.
3. The transferring office asks that the records be described as the "Weldon Harbor Community
   Development Collection."

DEED OF GIFT

Not executed. A draft was sent in December 2025 and returned with the signature block blank and a
note reading "with counsel." The draft does not address copyright, and no statement of the
Authority's own rights in the photographic material has been obtained. Two of the three conditions
above appear only in email and not in the draft deed.

POSSIBLE RESTRICTED PERSONAL INFORMATION

The relocation case files appear to be organized one folder per displaced household and to contain
names, home addresses, household composition, income statements, and correspondence with the
Authority. A sample of four folders included what appear to be physician's letters supporting
requests for rehousing, and one folder labeled "medical." A separate run of files is labeled
"employee grievances, 1963–1971." None of this material has been surveyed systematically.

PROCESSING CONTEXT

Three archivists, one of them half-time on instruction. Average across the last three years is
roughly 180 linear feet processed per year across all collections. Two other accessions are pending:
a 60-linear-foot literary collection accepted in 2024 with a donor expectation of a finished finding
aid, and an 18-linear-foot business collection with no conditions attached.

There is a probable overlap between the photographic negatives in this accession and the Tidal
Flats Photographic Survey (PH-041) already held and partly digitized. The extent of the duplication
is unknown.

An outside researcher has already asked for access to Tidal Flats material.

PRELIMINARY APPRAISAL - NOT A DECISION

Nothing has been appraised out. Nothing has been reformatted. Nothing is open. The accession is
recorded and shelved as received.
