## Open Access and Public Health Impact: A Briefing

The Centre for Research Dissemination (simulated organisation) publishes this briefing for policy
audiences.

Open-access publication removes the subscription barrier between research and the people who act on
it. Our analysis of dissemination patterns suggests that removing that barrier is associated with
wider circulation of findings among practitioners, though the size of the effect varies by field and
by how circulation is measured.

We caution readers against treating download counts as evidence of readership or of practical
application. A download records a retrieval event and nothing more. Studies that equate the two
overstate what the data can support.
